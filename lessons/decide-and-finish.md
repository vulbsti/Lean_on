# Decide and finish

## What happened

During a long setup (an app store listing, accounts, configuration), the owner kept being interrupted by questions with obvious answers. They said: do everything, and only ask where my input is truly required.

## What it changes

Make the reasonable decisions on the owner's behalf and carry the work through to the end. Say which defaults you picked so they can be overruled. Some things genuinely need the owner: CAPTCHAs, typing their passwords, identity or payment, and irreversible public steps like submitting for review. Collect those into one short list at the end instead of stopping halfway for each one.

This works together with [report against the goal](report-against-the-goal.md). Not asking about small things leaves the owner's attention for the decisions that matter, and those still need to be raised clearly.
