# Voice

Read before drafting. These are the user's own preferences and override the general guidance in SKILL.md where they differ.

## Known from past feedback

- Wants essays that read like Paul Graham, Sam Altman, Gwern or Signull: one person thinking a question through.
- Does not want the register of NYT or Bloomberg explainers.
- No closing line written to sound final. No opening that corrects a view the reader may not hold.
- No fixed word limit. Length is fine; padding is not.
- For posts on X: first person, a little messy, thinking out loud.

## In the user's own words

The reader needs to feel drawn in but also leave feeling they have got clarity. A good essay takes them on a journey and makes them feel engaged throughout, like they are discovering things themselves. But without artificial bloat.
